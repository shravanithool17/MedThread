# Related Work: Verified References and Scope

## Scope and method

This is a short orientation bibliography, not a systematic review. Bibliographic metadata and titles below were checked against Crossref/ACL Anthology records on 2026-10-27. The project has not performed exhaustive PubMed, ACL Anthology, IEEE, ACM, or software-repository searches. Therefore no research gap, priority, or novelty claim is established.

## Relevance and distinction

NegEx and ConText provide established context/negation-rule precedents. The i2b2/VA challenge demonstrates that clinical concept extraction, assertion classification, and relations are established benchmark tasks. Clinical TempEval is relevant to temporal event/expression extraction. MedThread is a small rule-based educational prototype; it does not reproduce those systems, use their datasets, or compare performance against them.

Longitudinal patient modeling and contradiction/consistency detection are adjacent research areas. This short list does not claim complete coverage of those topics. The project must not claim “first”, “novel”, or a confirmed research gap without a separately documented systematic search and comparison.
